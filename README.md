Welcome to Advanced Platformer (Part 3), a fast-paced Scratch platformer packed with new levels, enemies, collectibles, and visual upgrades!

🕹️ How to Play

• Move & Jump: Use the Arrow Keys to navigate across platforms.
• Goal: Collect all apples to complete the level.
• Keys & Doors: Find and collect keys to unlock doors blocking your path.
• Combat: Defeat as many enemies as you can to rack up your score on the kill tracker!

✨ What's New in This Version

• Expanded Level Design: Increased from 10 platforms to 30+ platforms.
• Enemies & Combat: Added new enemies distributed throughout the level, complete with an Enemy Kill Tracker.
• Health System: Added Hearts / Lives to raise the stakes.
• New Collectibles: More items to discover and gather along the way.
• UI & Visual Upgrades: Added a Main Menu, a You Win Menu, and a sleek blur effect.
• Audio Variety: Added multiple new background tracks to keep the energy high.
• Under the Hood: Scaled up to 1,200+ blocks of code (a massive jump from 734 blocks in the previous version!).
